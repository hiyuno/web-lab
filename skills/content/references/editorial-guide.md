## Conventions

- Address: formal | informal
- Numbers: digits from 10 up; "900 pesos" or "$900 MXN"; percentages with the symbol
- Dates: 5 September 2026 | 05/09/2026
- Capitalization: sentence case; proper nouns only
- Punctuation in buttons and headings: no final period
- Emoji: no | only in [context]
- Languages: [es-MX] · [en] · what is translated and what is not

## Microcopy

- Buttons: verb + object ("Request a quote", "See plans"). Never "Submit", "Click here", "More".
- Links: make sense out of context ("read the privacy notice", not "here").
- Form errors: what happened and how to fix it, next to the field.
- Confirmations: what happened and what comes next.
- Placeholders do not replace the field label.

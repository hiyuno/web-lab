## Style and clarity

- [ ] Follows the editorial guide: voice, tone by situation, conventions, controlled vocabulary
- [ ] The first sentence says what the page is and for whom
- [ ] Inverted pyramid: the important part on top
- [ ] Paragraphs of three lines or fewer; lists where there are more than two items
- [ ] No superlatives or internal jargon; every claim with proof and source
- [ ] Plain language: a hurried reader who does not know the sector gets it
- [ ] Half the words of paper; read aloud

## Content accessibility

- [ ] One H1; H2 and H3 in order without skipping; headings stand alone
- [ ] Link text meaningful out of context; never "click here"
- [ ] Alt text on images with content; `alt=""` on decorative ones
- [ ] Instructions that do not rely only on color, shape or position ("the green button on the right")
- [ ] Abbreviations explained the first time
- [ ] Form errors say what happened and how to fix it

## SEO

- [ ] Title 50-60, unique, keyword first
- [ ] Meta description 120-160 with a call to action
- [ ] H1 aligned with intent and keyword
- [ ] Direct 40-60-word answer at the top
- [ ] Internal links to pillar or satellites with descriptive anchors
- [ ] Template's JSON-LD with visible data
- [ ] OG image assigned in assets.md

## Facts and legal

- [ ] Testimonials with the person's permission
- [ ] Promises the architecture and the business keep
- [ ] Forms: every field with a reason; short notice next to the form

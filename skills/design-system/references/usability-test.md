# Usability test · [project]

Date: [yyyy-mm-dd] · Facilitator: [ ] · Prototype: [URL or branch] · Participants: [3 to 5, outside the project]

## Preparation

- Tasks from `docs/02-structure/flows.md`, written as a situation, not an instruction: "You want to know how much the beginners' class costs. Show me how you would find out." Never use the labels' words.
- Prototype on the participant's device if possible; otherwise a real phone at 375 and desktop.
- Twenty minutes per person. Record the screen with permission; do not record faces without explicit permission.

## Script

1. "We are not testing you, we are testing the design. There are no wrong answers."
2. "Think aloud: what you see, what you expect to happen, what confuses you."
3. One task at a time. Do not help. If they get stuck for more than a minute, ask "what did you expect to find?" and move to the next.
4. At the end: "What was the most confusing? What would you change? In one sentence, what is this site and for whom?"

## Interpretation rules

- A problem seen in 2 of 5 people is real. Fix it.
- If the problem is in a label or the structure, go back to Rosenfeld; do not cover it with design.
- If the problem is content (they do not understand what it is), go back to that page's brief.
- Fix in the system (component or token), not on the page, and retest only the tasks that failed.
